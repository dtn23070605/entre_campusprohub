How it works

Order in 3 simple steps

No queues. No confusion. Your document is ready when you arrive.

1

Upload your files online

Send your file through the booking form, Zalo, or our Google Form — before you leave home or between classes. We accept PDF, DOCX, PPTX, and image files.

2

We prepare your order

Staff confirm your order, print and bind everything, and notify you when it's ready. Usually within 1–2 hours for standard orders, or next-hour for priority.

3

Pick up and pay

Walk in, collect your documents at the counter, and pay by cash or bank transfer. No waiting in line. All uploaded files are deleted after pickup.
